Delete your Parklane account

Parklane is the resident app of the Park Lane compound, operated by {{OPERATOR}}. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account & security → Delete account) or here.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email and password, and every sign-in on your phones.
  • Your place in your apartment, and with it your access to its devices and gateway.
  • Your Health Passport: profiles, records, documents and summaries, and records you shared or that were shared with you.
  • Upcoming clinic appointments are cancelled.

What is kept

  • The clinic's record of visits that already happened (the patient's name as booked and the doctor's notes), which clinics must keep as medical records. It is no longer linked to your name or email in the app.
  • The building's log of device actions, no longer linked to you.
  • Brand accounts linked to your apartment's home hub belong to the apartment; remove them in the app first if you want them gone.

Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. See the privacy policy.

Can't sign in? Ask building management to reset your password, or write to {{CONTACT}} to request deletion.
